Learning goal: Name different vehicles, identify where they travel and describe them using simple English sentences.
WORLD 1

🛣️ Land Transport

Land transport includes vehicles that move on roads or railway tracks.

🚌

Bus

A bus has many seats. It carries people from one place to another.

🚗

Car

A car has four wheels. It carries people on roads.

🚲

Bicycle

A bicycle has two wheels. People ride it on roads or paths.

🚆

Train

A train has many coaches. It travels on railway tracks.

🏍️

Motorcycle

A motorcycle has two wheels. It travels on roads.

Say it! “This is a bus. It belongs to land transport.”
WORLD 2

☁️ Air Transport

Air transport includes vehicles that fly through the sky.

✈️

Aeroplane

An aeroplane has wings. It carries many passengers through the sky.

🚁

Helicopter

A helicopter has spinning blades. It can take off and land vertically.

🎈

Hot Air Balloon

A hot air balloon floats in the sky using heated air.

Say it! “This is an aeroplane. It belongs to air transport.”
WORLD 3

🌊 Water Transport

Water transport includes vehicles that travel on water or below its surface.

🚢

Ship

A ship is large. It carries people or goods across the sea.

⛵

Boat

A boat is smaller than many ships. It can travel across rivers and lakes.

🤿

Submarine

A submarine travels underwater. It can explore deep parts of the ocean.

Say it! “This is a ship. It belongs to water transport.”
